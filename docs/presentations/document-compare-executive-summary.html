The problem

Comparing contracts by hand is slow and risky

Reviewers need a redline they can trust — produced quickly, and without sending confidential documents to an outside AI model.

Slow

Reading two versions line by line takes hours that senior reviewers do not have.

Error-prone

Manual comparison misses subtle edits — exactly the changes that carry the most risk.

Confidential

Client documents should not be handed to a third-party model just to spot differences.

Scope

What the first version does — and does not

In scope now

  • Compare a Word document against another Word document.
  • Produce a native Word tracked-changes redline.
  • Show a structured, in-app view of every change.
  • Inline and side-by-side review, plus download.

Planned for later

  • Detecting moved text as a move (not delete plus insert).
  • Tracking formatting-only changes.
  • Comments, footnotes, images, headers, and footers.
  • Legacy Word and PDF inputs; three-way merge; AI suggestions.
Risks & mitigations

Known trade-offs, with a plan for each

Very large documents take longer, because the first version does the work while the user waits.

The design is already ready for a background worker as a fast-follow.

Only Word documents are supported; other file types could confuse users.

Unsupported files are rejected up front with a clear message.

The redline must open cleanly in both Microsoft Word and Google Docs.

Each result is re-checked automatically, with an optional render test.
